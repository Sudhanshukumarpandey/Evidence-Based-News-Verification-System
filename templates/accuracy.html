Key Evaluation Metrics Explained

Accuracy

The percentage of total predictions that were correct. $$\text{Accuracy} = \frac{\text{True Positives} + \text{True Negatives}}{\text{Total Predictions}}$$

Precision (Positive Predictive Value)

Out of all the news articles that the model predicted to be Real, how many were actually real? High precision is important if you want to avoid labeling fake articles as real news. $$\text{Precision} = \frac{\text{True Positives}}{\text{True Positives} + \text{False Positives}}$$

Recall (Sensitivity)

Out of all the news articles that were actually Real, how many did the model correctly identify? High recall means the model does not miss genuine news. $$\text{Recall} = \frac{\text{True Positives}}{\text{True Positives} + \text{False Negatives}}$$

F1 Score

The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. It provides a single balanced score, particularly useful if classes are imbalanced. $$\text{F1 Score} = 2 \times \frac{\text{Precision} \times \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}$$
